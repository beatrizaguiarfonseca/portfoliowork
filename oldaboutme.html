Living in different countries allowed me to learn various dance styles, and now I get to show that through Dale Duro, the Latin Dance Company at Northwestern

Dance Styles I Love:

  • • Reggaeton: Creates community and good energy through dance
  • • Salsa: Expression and connection through movement
  • • Bomba: Puerto Rican style dance
