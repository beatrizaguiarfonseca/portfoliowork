Overview

Repositioning C4 for the
everyday champion.

Our team was tasked with evaluating and reimagining the brand strategy of C4 Smart Energy — a clean energy drink competing with names like Celsius and Alani Nu. This project was conducted in collaboration with Heather Gasper, VP of Product Strategy at Nutrabolt, as part of Northwestern's ENTREP 395 course.

"How might we reposition C4 as the go-to clean energy drink for today's health-conscious, purpose-driven, and hype-sensitive consumer?"

We led a comprehensive analysis of the market, brand perception, and consumer behavior — ultimately delivering a brand refresh and campaign activation strategy anchored in functionality, personalization, and movement. The positioning we landed on: "Fueling the Everyday Champion."

Process

Research, strategy,
repositioning.

01
Market Research & Competitive Analysis

Competitive benchmarking: We mapped the clean energy market in depth — analyzing Red Bull, Monster, Celsius, Alani Nu, and Bang across four dimensions: visual identity, target audience, messaging strategy, and shelf positioning.

Consumer interviews: We spoke with college students and young professionals about their energy drink habits — what they reach for and why, what turns them off, and what "clean" actually means to them as a purchase signal.

Brand audit: We conducted a thorough audit of C4's existing visual identity, packaging, social presence, and messaging — identifying both equity worth preserving and the gaps creating friction with the target consumer.

Competitive research sessions — mapping the clean energy market and C4's positioning gap

02
Brand Strategy & Repositioning

Core positioning: Inspired by C4's existing athletic reach and clean formulation, we developed the positioning: "Fueling the Everyday Champion." This shifted the brand from a niche gym drink to an everyday performance companion — for the student pulling an all-nighter, the professional navigating back-to-back meetings, the athlete in the gym at 6am.

#Pop4Purpose campaign: We developed a hashtag campaign inviting users to write their mantra or daily goal directly on the can — transforming the product into a personal statement. The mechanic created social proof, UGC, and a direct line between the consumer's identity and the product.

#M4rkableMoments: A companion campaign highlighting real moments of personal achievement — designed to build community and emotional connection beyond the product itself.

Can redesign concepts: Proposed minimalist can redesigns prioritizing visual clarity and shelf appeal — cleaner typography, reduced noise, and a more confident color hierarchy that could compete directly with Celsius's shelf presence.

03
Final Presentation to Nutrabolt

We built our final deliverable in Beautiful.ai — a full brand strategy presentation including our research findings, positioning framework, campaign concepts, and visual redesign recommendations. The deck was presented to Heather Gasper, VP of Product Strategy at Nutrabolt, in a live Q&A session.

Final brand strategy presentation — delivered to Nutrabolt's VP of Product Strategy

Reflections

What I learned.

Working on the C4 brand pushed us to blend consumer empathy with bold brand moves. From understanding the clean energy niche to delivering a market-ready repositioning, the experience sharpened my ability to synthesize research into strategy — and strategy into stories.

Collaborating directly with an industry partner changed the stakes of the work. We weren't just writing a class paper — we were making real recommendations to a real VP at a real company. That pressure made the thinking sharper, the arguments cleaner, and the final presentation something I was genuinely proud to defend.
