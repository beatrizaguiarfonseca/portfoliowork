Overview

Safety technology that empowers,
not surveils.

As part of the Upkey Virtual Internship Program, I joined an international team of students to ideate, prototype, and pitch a technology-based solution to a pressing global issue. We created THERE4YOU — a discreet, smart bracelet built to protect individuals from abuse, violence, and harassment through innovative 4-button safety technology.

"How might we protect vulnerable individuals in dangerous moments while making them feel empowered, not monitored?"

The product and pitch won 1st place out of 700+ global projects in the IE Business School Venture Lab Series, praised for its vision, feasibility, and emotional resonance. I led the brand identity, UX concepts, and multi-platform marketing strategy.

Process

Designing with empathy first.

01
Research & Problem Definition

Who we were designing for: People who regularly navigate situations where they feel unsafe — but don't have a discreet, dignified way to call for help. Existing solutions fell into two camps: obvious panic buttons that feel stigmatizing, or apps that require unlocking your phone in a stressful moment.

Key design principle: The product needed to feel like jewelry, not a security device. It needed to work with a single gesture. And it needed to protect dignity — not just safety.

Market gap: No wearable product on the market combined silent SOS alerts, audio documentation, and live location sharing in a form factor people would actually want to wear every day.

02
Product: The 4-Button Bracelet

We designed THERE4YOU around four core safety interactions — all accessible from a discreet wrist device, requiring no phone unlock and no visible distress signal.

1
Power On/Off
Discretely activate or deactivate the device — wearable as everyday jewelry with no visible indicator of its function.
2
Loud Sound Deterrent
Emit a loud alarm to deter immediate threats — creating space and drawing attention without requiring the user to speak.
3
Alert Police Station
Automatically send a GPS-tagged alert to the nearest police station — no call required, no escalation needed from the user.
4
SOS to Emergency Contacts
Instantly send a live location and "SOS" message to pre-selected trusted contacts — a quiet signal that says "I need you."

THERE4YOU bracelet — 4-button safety technology designed to wear like jewelry

03
Brand Identity & Marketing Strategy

Naming: "THERE4YOU" communicates presence, reliability, and care. It's the message you want from a friend, not a product — which was exactly the emotional positioning we needed.

Visual identity: Warm, empowering tones — not clinical or fear-driven. Safety products typically lean dark and heavy; we deliberately went the opposite direction to make THERE4YOU feel approachable and wearable.

Marketing concepts: Developed multi-platform marketing strategy covering social media, influencer partnerships, and campus outreach — targeting college students and young professionals as the primary demographic.

04
Global Pitch & Recognition

We developed and delivered the full pitch for the IE Business School Venture Lab Series, competing against 700+ projects from students across the globe. Our presentation covered the product design, market opportunity, go-to-market strategy, and financial projections.

The team and mentors worked through multiple rounds of feedback sessions, refining the concept and the pitch until it was both emotionally resonant and commercially viable.

Reflections

What I learned.

This project was a turning point — blending social impact with product innovation. It taught me how to pitch with clarity, design with empathy, and lead a team from concept to global recognition. THERE4YOU wasn't just a bracelet. It was proof that thoughtful design can literally save lives.

Working on a product with genuinely high stakes shaped every design decision I made. You can't design a safety product the same way you'd design a productivity app. Every interaction has to earn trust, reduce friction, and leave the user feeling more capable — not more afraid.
