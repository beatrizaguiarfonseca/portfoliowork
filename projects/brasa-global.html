Overview

Uniting 10,000+ students
through content.

As Social Media Lead for BRASA Global, I managed the digital presence for the largest network of Brazilian students abroad — 40K+ followers, thousands of accounts in monthly reach, and a community spanning 100+ cities worldwide. I directed strategy, created engaging campaigns, and tracked performance weekly to elevate BRASA's voice and visibility.

"How can we use content to unite 10,000+ Brazilian students in 100+ cities worldwide — and inspire the next generation of global leaders?"

I led weekly editorial planning, campaign development, and content production: briefs, design supervision, posting, and insights reporting — consistently driving high engagement and brand alignment across platforms.

Process

How I ran
the strategy.

01
Editorial Strategy & Content Planning

Weekly editorial planning: I built and managed a rolling content calendar across Instagram, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp — balancing recurring content pillars (student spotlights, chapter highlights, opportunities, and cultural moments) with reactive content tied to current events and BRASA initiatives.

Caption strategy: Wrote all captions with a bilingual-aware voice — resonant in both Portuguese and English, with a tone that felt personal and community-driven rather than institutional.

02
Content Production & Visual Storytelling

Design supervision: Briefed and reviewed all visual assets — ensuring consistency with BRASA's brand while allowing individual campaign personalities to shine. Collaborated with the creative team on templates, color systems, and post formats.

Campaign development: Developed dedicated campaign strategies for BRASA milestones — Summit promotion, scholarship announcements, application seasons, and cultural celebrations — each with its own visual identity and content arc.

BRASA Global content across campaigns — feed posts, Reels, analytics, and storytelling moments

03
Analytics, Reporting & Optimization

Weekly KPI reporting: Analyzed Instagram Insights weekly — tracking reach, impressions, saves, shares, and follower growth — and presented findings to the global marketing team with clear action recommendations for the following week.

Performance optimization: Used data to iterate on posting time, content format mix, and caption length — continuously improving the account's organic performance throughout the year.

Reflections

What I learned.

Working on BRASA's global comms was a masterclass in scale, collaboration, and creative discipline. I learned how to translate mission into message — and data into direction. Managing content for a global audience at the same time I was leading the Summit campaign (see BRASA Summit 2024) made this one of the most intense and formative years of my career.
