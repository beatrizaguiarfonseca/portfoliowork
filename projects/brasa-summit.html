Overview

The biggest Brazilian Student Association
event in the US — ever.

BRASA (Brazilian Student Association) is the largest network of Brazilian students in the world, with chapters at over 200 universities. Every year, BRASA hosts its flagship Summit, bringing together students, professionals, and leaders from across the Brazilian diaspora for networking, panels, and cultural celebration.

As a Global Board Member and Marketing Campaigns Analyst, I led the entire communications strategy for the BRASA Summit Américas 2024, our largest conference yet. From campaign kickoff in May to on-site content during the event in October, I managed both creative direction and technical execution across every channel.

How do you turn a student-led conference into an international brand with real influence, and sell it out for the first time in the organization's history?

The answer was making Brazilian students at US universities feel like this event was built for them, not just marketed at them. Every piece of content, every push, every telemarketing script was designed to close the gap between "that sounds cool" and "I already bought my ticket."

Process

How we built the campaign.

01
Strategy & Content Calendar

Multi-channel calendar: Built a content calendar across Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Facebook, with distinct messaging and formats for each platform. Instagram focused on visual storytelling and Reels; LinkedIn targeted sponsors and young professionals; email and telemarketing drove registration conversions.

Speaker spotlight series: Created a recurring content format featuring confirmed speakers and alumni, giving people a reason to follow along week by week and building anticipation. Each post was designed to generate saves and shares, not just likes.

Social media management: Produced content daily, monitored analytics in real time, and iterated rapidly based on what was driving clicks vs. what was just driving passive engagement.

Campaign visuals and analytics, designed for reach, optimized for conversion

02
Paid Amplification & Telemarketing

Meta Ads targeting: Ran targeted Meta campaigns reaching Brazilian students at top US universities, using custom audiences built from chapter follower lists and lookalike audiences based on past registrants.

Telemarketing outreach: Managed a telemarketing effort that reached 50,000+ contacts across the BRASA chapter network, personally scripting outreach to maximize conversion without feeling impersonal.

Early bird & deadline urgency: Structured the registration window with tiered pricing, each communicated with dedicated social pushes. The early-bird deadline drove a significant single-weekend spike in registrations.

03
On-Site Coverage & Speaker Logistics

Live social coverage: Managed real-time content throughout the Summit, capturing keynotes, panel moments, and candid community connections to keep the online community engaged in the experience in real time.

Speaker relations: Coordinated all on-site speaker logistics and team management, ensuring the event day ran smoothly behind the scenes while the front-of-house experience felt effortless.

Photo & video production: Directed photo and video coverage throughout the event, collecting the assets that would fuel the post-summit campaign cycle.

BRASA Summit Américas 2024, Columbia University, New York City

04
Post-Summit Campaign & Wrap

Recap content series: Produced a full post-summit content series, highlight reels, quote graphics, and a LinkedIn wrap-up post that drove the highest single-post engagement of the entire campaign cycle.

Instagram community: The post-event campaign extended the Summit's energy for weeks after, with attendee reposts, speaker quotes, and community celebration content maintaining momentum well past October.

Reflections

What I learned.

This campaign taught me that the most powerful marketing isn't about shouting louder, it's about making people feel genuinely seen. The 520% surge didn't happen because we ran more ads. It happened because we made Brazilian students at US universities feel like the Summit was built for them.

Leading an end-to-end campaign, from strategy through creative production to paid execution, telemarketing, live coverage, and post-event wrap, gave me a complete picture of what it actually takes to move people to action at scale. And doing it for a community I'm part of made every piece of content feel personal, not transactional.
