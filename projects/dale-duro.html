Overview

Translating rhythm
into graphic form.

Dale Duro is Northwestern's Latin dance company — one of the most beloved and high-energy student organizations on campus. Since joining the PR committee in Spring 2024, I've been leading design direction and promotional strategy for performances, auditions, and showcases.

How do you translate rhythm, energy, and cultura into graphic form — and make every flyer feel like a countdown to something worth showing up for?

My work spans Instagram promo design, in-show graphics, photography direction, photo editing, telemarketing outreach, and overall visual cohesion for the brand. From high-energy show campaigns to elegant audition posts, I collaborate closely with the team to make every performance feel like an event.

Process

Campaigns, season by season.

01
Spring Show 2025 — Record Attendance Campaign

End-to-end campaign leadership: Coordinated the full marketing campaign for Spring Show 2025, driving record attendance of 1,000+ students — the biggest Dale Duro show in recent memory.

Photoshoot direction: Planned and directed the show's promotional photoshoot — concept, location, talent direction, and post-production editing to create the campaign's hero visuals.

Ticket sales strategy: Built a tiered urgency strategy around ticket release windows — early bird, general, and door pricing — with dedicated social pushes at each stage to drive conversions.

Telemarketing outreach: Personally scripted and coordinated peer outreach across Northwestern dorms, clubs, and group chats to push ticket sales in the final two weeks before the show.

Spring Show 2025 campaign visuals — photoshoot direction, editing, and promotional assets

02
Refusionshaka — Show Visual Identity

Campaign concept & design: Led the design and rollout of the Refusionshaka show campaign — a high-energy visual identity built around the show's fusion concept, combining bold typography, vibrant colors, and motion-inspired graphics.

Consistency across formats: Designed assets across every touchpoint — Instagram posts, Stories, event banners, and physical flyers — maintaining a cohesive visual language from announcement through show night.

Refusionshaka campaign — full visual identity across all formats

03
Ongoing Brand Collateral — Auditions, Banners & Announcements

Audition campaigns: Designed the full suite of audition-season assets — info session graphics, countdown posts, and expectation-setting visuals that made auditions feel exciting and approachable rather than intimidating.

Seasonal brand consistency: Built a design system Dale Duro could use across seasons — consistent color use, typography hierarchy, and visual tone that immediately reads as Dale Duro without being rigid or repetitive.

Event photography: Supported in-stage visuals and photography during live events, capturing the energy of performances for use in post-show content and future campaign cycles.

Event banners and audition campaign assets

Reflections

What I learned.

Designing for Dale Duro is pure joy — vibrant, loud, fast-paced, and full of expression. It's about honoring Latin culture while turning every flyer and post into a bold celebration. From behind-the-scenes planning to spotlight design moments, this role pushes me creatively and culturally every single season.

Leading the Spring Show 2025 campaign end-to-end — from photoshoot concept to telemarketing to show-night content — taught me how much of a real marketing job lives in the unglamorous logistics. The 1,000+ turnout wasn't just the design. It was the follow-through.
