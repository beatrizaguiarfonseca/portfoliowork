Overview

A startup transformation in 10 weeks.

As a core member of WingRep's GTM team (formerly SelfActualize.AI), I directed the end-to-end rebrand and launch of the company's new identity. WingRep is an AI-powered sales coaching platform that integrates directly into reps' calendars, meeting tools, and deal workflows — built for the modern B2B sales team.

My work spanned rethinking the company's name, logo, and positioning from scratch, building a fundraising- and enterprise-ready website, and orchestrating a multi-channel content rollout that drove real traction with investors and early customers.

I managed brand strategy, design execution, and product storytelling to make WingRep VC-ready, market-aligned, and customer-facing — all while moving fast enough to deliver a completely new company identity inside a single summer.

Process

How I built it.

01
Rebrand: From SelfActualize.AI to WingRep

Naming + Logo + Tagline + Trademark: Directed competitive audits, brainstorming sessions, and trademark checks to rename the company to WingRep — signaling empowerment, growth, and action for sales reps. Designed the final logo in Figma through multiple rounds of founder feedback.

Brand Identity & Visual Guide: Built the full visual identity system: color palette, typography, iconography, and motion guidelines. Delivered a brand guide to ensure consistency across website, pitch decks, and all marketing assets.

Market Research & Focus Groups: Conducted competitor benchmarking against Gong, CoPilot, and Delphi, and interviewed SDRs and AEs to align positioning with real sales pain points: coaching, deal progression, and team motivation.

Before — SelfActualize.AI: generic AI branding, unclear positioning

After — WingRep: sales-focused, empowering, action-oriented

02
Website: wingrep.ai ↗

Structure & Messaging: Designed site architecture to serve both enterprise buyers and VC audiences simultaneously. Crafted messaging frameworks around three core personas: Sales Reps/SDRs, Sales Leaders, and Enablement Teams.

Design & Development: Created high-fidelity mockups in Figma, then implemented directly in Webflow — including custom graphics, micro-interactions, and animations to bring the product story to life.

Content & Positioning: Integrated real screenshots, product diagrams, and feature callouts. Built dedicated sections for security (SOC2 readiness), integrations (Zoom, Teams, Meet), and ROI for each buyer persona.

Four sections of the redesigned wingrep.ai — Home, About, Enablement, and Q&A

03
Rebrand Launch Campaign & Social Content

Final SelfActualize.AI Episodes: Produced, edited, and distributed final podcast/video episodes, testimonial reels, and founder spotlights under the old brand — bringing that chapter to a close with strong engagement.

Rebrand Announcement: Coordinated the official "SelfActualize.AI is now WingRep" LinkedIn campaign, crafting all copy and visuals to highlight company momentum and the vision behind the new name.

Creative Production: Designed branded social graphics, edited launch videos (Premiere Pro, Veo, CapCut), and produced carousel posts highlighting customer testimonials and product value props.

Business Impact

Results that moved the needle.

Fundraising

The website and brand materials directly supported $600K+ in angel funding secured during the rebrand period. Investors cited the clarity of the new positioning and the professionalism of the digital presence.

Customer Growth

Onboarded 5 new enterprise customers and reached $20K+ ARR during my internship — demonstrating that the rebrand resonated with the target sales team audience from day one.

Reflections

What I learned.

This project taught me how to combine brand strategy, UX/UI design, and marketing execution under real startup pressure. I learned to balance the needs of founders, investors, and customers while moving fast enough to deliver a new name, brand, website, and content ecosystem in just 10 weeks.

WingRep was more than a design sprint — it was a crash course in bringing a SaaS company to market with both speed and soul. The experience showed me how powerful cohesive branding can be in supporting business objectives, from fundraising to customer acquisition. I left the summer knowing I can own an entire brand transformation end-to-end.
