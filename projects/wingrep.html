WingRep: Complete Rebrand & Go-to-Market Launch

From SelfActualize.AI to WingRep — a full startup transformation including logo, website, and content ecosystem that supported $600K+ funding and 5 new enterprise customers.

Time Frame

Jun 2025 – Aug 2025

Location

San Francisco, CA

Role

Project Management, Marketing & Sales Intern

Team

Founders & GTM Team

Tools

Figma Webflow Adobe Suite Google Analytics ChatGPT

Overview

As a core member of WingRep's GTM team (formerly SelfActualize.AI), I directed the end-to-end rebrand and launch of the company's new identity. WingRep is an AI-powered sales coaching platform that integrates directly into reps' calendars, meeting tools, and deal workflows.

Over 10 weeks, I managed brand strategy, design execution, and product storytelling to make WingRep VC-ready, market-aligned, and customer-facing.

My work spanned rethinking the company's name, logo, and positioning from scratch, building a fundraising- and enterprise-ready website, and orchestrating a multi-channel content rollout that drove traction on LinkedIn and with early customers.

Key Results

  • • $600K+ in angel funding secured
  • • 5 new enterprise customers onboarded
  • • $20K+ ARR reached during internship
  • • 8,483 LinkedIn impressions (+39% growth)
  • • Brand-new set of promotional materials and social media content, with a unified visual identity.

Process Breakdown

1

Rebrand: From SelfActualize.AI to WingRep

Naming + Logo + Tagline + Trademark: Directed competitive audits, brainstorming sessions, and trademark checks to rename the company to WingRep, signaling empowerment, growth, and action for sales reps. Designed the final logo in Figma with iterative feedback from founders.

Brand Identity & Visual Guide: Built the full visual identity system: color palette (#029244, #5c4bca, #F08B4F), typography, iconography, and motion guidelines. Delivered a brand guide to ensure consistency across website, decks, and marketing assets.

Market Research & Focus Groups: Conducted competitor benchmarking (Gong, CoPilot, Delphi) and interviewed SDRs and AEs to align positioning with real sales pain points: coaching, deal progression, and team motivation.

2

Website: wingrep.ai

Structure & Messaging: Designed site architecture to serve both enterprise buyers and VC audiences. Crafted messaging frameworks and strategic communication plans around three core personas: Sales Reps/SDRs, Sales Leaders, and Enablement Teams.

Design & Development: Created high-fidelity mockups in Figma, then implemented directly in Webflow, including graphics, micro-interactions, and animations to bring the product story to life.

Content & Positioning: Integrated real screenshots, product diagrams, and feature callouts for reps, managers, and investors. Built dedicated sections for security (SOC2 readiness), integrations (Zoom, Teams, Meet), and ROI.

3

Social Media Announcement & Content

Final SelfActualize.AI Podcast Episodes: Produced, edited, and distributed final podcast/video episodes, testimonial reels, and founder spotlights under the old name.

Rebrand Launch Campaign: Coordinated the official "SelfActualize.AI is now WingRep" LinkedIn announcement, crafting copy and visuals that highlighted the company's growth.

Content Performance (Aug 2025):
  • • 2,505 impressions (+189.9%), 84 reactions, 12 comments, 6 reposts in 2 weeks
  • • 8,483 impressions (+39%), 272 reactions, 33 comments, 18 reposts across 2 months
  • • 384 page views and 155 unique visitors in August (+5.2%)

Creative Production: Designed branded social graphics, edited launch videos (Premiere Pro, Veo, CapCut), and produced carousel posts highlighting customer testimonials and product value.

4

Promotional Materials

Stickers & Merch: Designed branded stickers and merchandise to build culture internally and provide shareable assets at events.

Business Cards: Created professional cards aligned with the new WingRep identity for founders and GTM team to use at fundraising and networking events.

Marketing Collateral: Delivered flyers, templates, and pitch deck visuals with a consistent brand voice across all print and digital channels.
