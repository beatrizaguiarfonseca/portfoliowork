Overview

Making global education
accessible for every student.

The International Study Ambassador Program is a youth-led global education initiative helping students pursue international studies. ISAP has impacted 6.5M+ people with 47K+ followers across social platforms — and I helped build that reach from the inside.

I began as an ambassador and grew into VP and Country Manager within a year, overseeing content creation, community strategy, social engagement, and ambassador coordination across Brazil. Our mission was to provide high-quality educational content in an engaging, youth-driven format — meeting students where they already were: on Instagram and TikTok.

"Make global education feel possible — not aspirational and distant, but real, achievable, and meant for you."

Process

From ambassador
to Country Manager.

01
Content Strategy & Social Media Growth

Reels & TikTok strategy: I scripted, recorded, and edited Reels and TikToks explaining college applications to students from 10+ countries — in plain language, with formats designed for the short-attention-span reality of our audience. This drove a tripling of engagement and 8,000 new followers.

SEO-optimized captions: Created captions designed to be found — using keyword research to ensure ISAP's content surfaced when Brazilian students searched for international study guidance.

ISAP social media content — designed to explain international study options clearly and engagingly

02
Team Leadership & Department Restructuring

As Country Manager: I led a restructuring of ISAP's marketing department and board operations — creating clearer role definitions, a more efficient content production pipeline, and better cross-team communication. I delegated tasks to 20+ team members, developed content calendars, and tracked performance analytics weekly.

Ambassador coordination: Managed a team of 30+ volunteers across Brazil, ensuring consistent content quality and mission alignment across all regional accounts.

03
Strategic Partnerships

Forged partnerships with Crimson Education, GEDUC, +MAIS, and other major players in education access — expanding ISAP's credibility and reach while providing genuine value to our student audience through co-produced content and joint campaigns.

Reflections

What I learned.

Leading and growing a youth-led organization across time zones taught me not just how to market — but how to lead with empathy, make impact scalable, and speak directly to what our audience needed. This experience shaped my understanding of what makes digital content meaningful and effective.

Going from ambassador to Country Manager in 16 months also taught me something about myself: I move fast when I care about the mission. And I really cared about this one.
